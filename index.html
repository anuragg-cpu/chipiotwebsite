The Problem

A recording is evidence after the fact. It isn’t help in the moment.

A resident falls in the stairwell. A senior citizen collapses, alone, at home. A security guard’s phone has no signal in the basement parking. Every existing setup has the same silence at exactly the wrong moment.

01

CCTV watches, it doesn’t call

Cameras and intercoms only work if someone is watching the right screen or able to reach a handset right when it happens. Most of the time, no one is.

02

Apps need a phone, signal, and a free hand

A mobile app depends on a charged phone, an unlocked screen, and network signal — three things that are hardest to guarantee in an actual emergency.

03

Staffed hours end. Risk doesn’t

Reception, facility desks, and community managers go home by evening — exactly when basements, stairwells, and unstaffed floors are most exposed.

How ABHAY Works

Press. Alert. Respond.

ABHAY is a wireless emergency alert button. Press it, and a designated responder is alerted instantly — no phone, no app, no dependency on the building’s Wi-Fi or a working handset.

1. Press

A resident or staff member presses the ABHAY button — wall-mounted in-flat, in a common area, or worn as a pendant.

Sub-GHz RF Signal

2. Alert

The signal reaches the receiver panel at the security or facility desk instantly, over a dedicated wireless network — not the building’s Wi-Fi.

Local Alert

3. Respond

Security or a designated responder is dispatched, knowing exactly which button, floor, or zone the alert came from.

Why Offline-First Matters

No internet. No mobile network. No dependency.

ABHAY runs on its own dedicated sub-GHz wireless network — not the building’s WiFi, and not a resident’s phone signal. It keeps working exactly when WiFi- and app-based alternatives are most likely to fail: during a power cut, a network outage, or after hours.
